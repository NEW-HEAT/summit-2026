    * Main crew agintel.ai (2min)
        Around the same time I started building VisualPT - I got hired by a startup serving citrus farmers and insurers around the Americas.
        When I came in - the company was using Leaflet and had a functional tool that customers were using to analyze groves -  10's to 100,000's of trees.         The catch was that the codebase was built in PHP in a few 5000 line files mixing portuguese and english - I was responsible for the rebuild 
        I quickly realized how much of a bottleneck using Leaflet was above a few thousand items.
        I found a GPU-driven package called Leaflet.glify and put it to use - but found a ton of issues with it. 
        When I checked the repo - I found it had been unmaintained for years and had accumulated numerous issues and PRs.          This was my first big open source moment - I stacked as many PRs as I could and linked as many issues to them, and opened a catchall PR.
        This got the attention of the owner, and merged, but soon after revealed many more cracks in the system that left me exploring more mature renderers.
        Remembering deck.gl, I quickly pivoted and have since been using it as the primary mapping system, stacking a new TreeLayer on top.
